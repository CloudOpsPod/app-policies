Classic Family Games – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Classic Family Games ("the app") is a free collection of offline board and puzzle games. The short version: the app collects no personal data and sends nothing off your device.

What stays on your device

  • Player names you type, characters you choose, scores and settings (such as auto-rotate) are saved locally on your device only.
  • Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes this data.

What we do not do

  • We do not require an account.
  • We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs, and we do not sell or share personal data.
  • The app does not request camera, microphone, contacts, location or storage permissions.

Device features used

The app uses your device's built-in text-to-speech engine for read-aloud instructions and character voices, and vibration for feedback. This happens on your device.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes (for example, if optional purchases are added), the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date.
