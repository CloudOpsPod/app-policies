IMG Text Searcher – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

IMG Text Searcher ("the app") lets you search your own images by the text and barcodes inside them. This policy explains what the app does with your data. The short version: your images and the text found in them stay on your device.

What the app processes

  • Images you choose. You pick images using Android's gallery or file picker. The app reads the text and barcodes in them using Google ML Kit, which runs entirely on your device.
  • Scanned text and image copies. To make search possible, the app stores a copy of each image you import and the text it found in private app storage on your device. This data is never uploaded to us or anyone else.

What we do not do

  • We do not upload your images or scanned text to any server.
  • We do not require an account.
  • We do not use advertising or analytics SDKs, and we do not sell or share your personal data.
  • The app does not request camera, contacts, location or broad storage permissions.

Subscriptions

Premium is an optional subscription sold through Google Play. Payments are handled by Google Play. We use RevenueCat (revenuecat.com) to verify whether a subscription is active; RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and purchase information, but not your images or scanned text. See RevenueCat's privacy policy for how it handles that data.

Your control

  • Remove a single image from the index from the preview screen, or delete everything via Settings → Delete all indexed data.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes all data it stored on your device.
  • Your original photos are never modified or deleted by the app.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.
